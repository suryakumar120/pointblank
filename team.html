Our Advisors

Harish Natarajan Natarajan
Harish is the Founder and Director at HxCo India (the Healthcare Collective). HxCo dia- logues with the government , insurance companies and provider groups to drive inno- vative models in healthcare – with the intent move from conventional “cure” oriented models to more proactive models with a holistic, community based approach. Harish is on the selection committee of IIT Madras’s Biotech incubator and is on the mentor board of India Accelerator’s IA Pulse. He has been an instructor with the Gopalakrishna Deshpande Center for Innovation at IIT Madras. Harish was the founding CEO of HCL Avitas, an initiative promoted by Shiv Nadar and supported by Johns Hopkins to develop a “primary care at scale” offering across India. Prior to HCL Avitas, most of his career was spent working for multinationals in India and South East Asia – the last stint being Managing Director-India, Thailand and Indo China for Bausch + Lomb. In addition to delivering business objectives in every stint, Harish has also been at the forefront of several initiatives that aim at improving the quality of healthcare in India. An Engineer from IIT Madras and an MBA from IIM Ahmedabad, Harish’s diverse expe- rience of 35 years include stints in Beverages (Pepsico), Paints (ICI Dulux) and Agro- chemicals.

Our Partners

Pointblank has a strategic partnership with Systech USA, renowned for its state-of-the-art data analytics platforms and AI capabilities. With a proven track record of delivering innovative solutions to healthcare providers globally, Systech USA provides the technological backbone to Pointblank for delivering AI/ML/Data Analytics solutions tailor-made to challenges faced by players in the Indian healthcare sector. These solutions empower healthcare organisations to make informed decisions, optimise resource allocation, and enhance patient engagement.

HxCo (the Healthcare Collective) has its primary purpose to promote good health and foster healthy communities. Pointblank partners with HxCo to help international healthcare players, start-ups and innovators launch, accelerate and grow their presence in India, and to build business cases for population health interventions.

Pointblank partners with Sqarona to provide end-to-end medical writing services including manuscripts, abstracts, posters, and oral presentations. We adhere to the widely accepted publication practices and guidelines including Good Publication Practice (GPP3), Declaration of Helsinki, International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) recommendations, and various reporting guidelines (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, CARE, etc).
